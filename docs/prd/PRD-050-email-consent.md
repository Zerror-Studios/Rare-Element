## What the shopper sees (Nahara)

- **`/email/confirm?token=…`** (`app/email/confirm/page.jsx` + `confirm-client.jsx`): the link in the confirmation email. The token is stripped from the address bar at once and kept in memory; states "Confirming…", subscribed, already confirmed, expired (7 days: "sign up again"), link not valid (wrong, or withdrawn since), try later and connection problem (both with Try again). `noindex`, disallowed in `app/robots.js`, no referrer (kit proxy).
- **`/email/preferences?token=…`** (`app/email/preferences/page.jsx` + `preferences-client.jsx`): the link in every email. Shows the masked address and "News and offers: Subscribed / Waiting for your confirmation / Not subscribed"; Unsubscribe asks once more ("Emails about your orders will still reach you"), Subscribe again for an unsubscribed address; saved, rate-limited and expired-link states. A reload shows "link not valid" (the token is read once): open the email's link again.
- **Account** (`components/account/settings/EmailPreference.jsx` (Account › Settings)`): the store's real consent from `myEmailPreferences`, never ticked for the shopper, labelled "Email me news and offers from Nahara" with "We'll email you a link to confirm first"; saving sends the notice version `nahara-email-2026-11-27`; a subscription shows "Waiting for your confirmation" until the click; `NO_EMAIL`, rate-limit and load errors with Try again.
- **Newsletter** (`components/common/NewsletterForm.jsx` (footer)`): the kit's `useEmailSignup` (double opt-in, hidden honeypot field, page throttle); after a sign-up "Check your inbox: we've sent you a link to confirm your subscription" (the same for an address already on the list), a line under the field says a confirmation comes first, and "Too many tries. Please try again in N minutes" on a limit.
- **Checkout** (`components/checkout/ContactDetail.jsx` (signed in) and `GuestContactDetail.jsx` (guest)`): the news box names the store, starts unticked (the schema default is now UNSUBSCRIBED, FR-3.8), and once ticked says "We'll email you a link to confirm first".
- `/unsubscribe` is unchanged: its links carry the older newsletter token, which the preference page can't read.
- Look: the status card (`status_section`, green buttons) of the back-in-stock confirmation page; mobile-first; every control has a label; no analytics event and no address in analytics from these pages.

## Email attribution and browse data (FR-4.4, FR-5.5; kit 2.1.0, 2026-11-27)

- **Browse abandonment:** the product page (`src/app/products/[slug]/product-client.jsx`) calls the kit's `useRecordProductView(data?._id)`: the view reaches the backend's `recordProductView` only for a signed-in shopper who chose **Analytics** in the cookie banner (the banner's "understand how the site is used"), once per product per browser session, after 2 s on the page; never for a guest; fire and forget (nothing shown, nothing blocks).
- **Email attribution:** nothing in the store: the kit keeps a landing from Nahara's campaign or automation emails (`utm_content=zm.<message id>`) 3 days with analytics consent and the checkout (`useCheckout`) sends it in `OrderAttributionInput`; the token leaves the address once read.
- **No token reaches GA4, GTM or the Pixel:** every tag loads through the kit's `ZcomTags`, which waits until a token page (`/email/confirm`, `/email/preferences`, `/unsubscribe`, `/review`, `/review/unsubscribe`, `/back-in-stock/confirm`, `/reset-password`) has stripped its query, and loads nothing on `/pay/<token>` or `/orders/status/<token>`. `test/analytics-privacy.test.mjs` pins it: the product page wiring, no tag script in the store's own code, and every token page stripping its query.
- **Sign-up forms (FR-3.8, D20):** when Nahara has an ACTIVE inline form (dashboard Marketing › Forms), the footer (`components/common/NewsletterForm.jsx`) shows it instead of the newsletter form: the kit's `useStorefrontSignupForms("INLINE")` + `pickSignupForm` find the newest one and `useSignupForm(form._id, { form })` renders its heading, description, optional first name, button text and consent text (sent back with its notice version) in the footer's own styling (the form's colours are not applied); after a sign-up it shows the form's success message (uniform for every address), with the same honeypot and page throttle, "Too many tries…" on a limit, and its own messages for a missing or too-long first name. With no form, while it loads, on an error, or once the form is paused (`unavailable`), the footer keeps the `useEmailSignup` newsletter form. Copy in `src/utils/email-consent.js` (`getSignupFormMessage`).
- **Pop-ups, footer forms and views (D22, kit 2.1.0 fourth pass):**
  - **Footer:** the store's newest ACTIVE **FOOTER** form, else its newest INLINE one (kit `pickFooterSignupForm` over `useStorefrontSignupForms(null)`, the same request as the pop-up's), as before in the footer's own styling; the form counts one view per browser session once half of it is on screen (`ref={useSignupFormView(form._id)}`). `FOOTER_SIGNUP_FORM_KIND` was removed.
  - **Pop-up:** `components/common/SignupPopup.jsx`, rendered once by `components/layouts/Layout.jsx`, shows the store's ACTIVE POPUP form as an accessible modal on the kit's `useSignupPopup` (merchant rules, consent-banner wait, delay, exit intent on computers only, focus trap and return, Escape and backdrop only for "button or outside", scroll lock without a layout shift, the view on open) and `useSignupForm` (labelled email and optional first name, honeypot, consent text with the confirmation hint, the success message in a live region, then "Continue shopping"). Styling: src/styles/components/common/signupPopup.css, a bottom sheet on phones and a centred card from 750 px, Nahara's green (#174d38) and white as fallbacks; the merchant's colours, corner radius and alignment come in through `--zc-signup-*` (only those tokens, `getSignupPopupStyle`), the brand fonts stay; no animation with reduced motion. `window.lenis` is stopped while it is open and the backdrop carries `data-lenis-prevent`.
  - **Pages:** `utils/signup-popup.js` maps routes to the kit's page types for the merchant's targeting: `/` HOME, `/products/<slug>` PRODUCT, any other top-level path that isn't a fixed route (the `[...categories]` catch-all) COLLECTION, `/checkout` CHECKOUT, `/account` ACCOUNT, `/orders` and `/pay` ORDER; besides the kit's blocked list (checkout, cart, account, order, pay and email token pages) it never shows on `/login`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`, `/review`, `/back-in-stock`. Tests `test/signup-popup.test.mjs`.
